Privacy policy

Tipsheet stores the shifts, jobs and settings you enter on your iPhone. The app requires no developer account and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking. The developer operates no server for your records.

iCloud and Apple services

When iCloud is available and sync is enabled, your ledger syncs through the private CloudKit database in your Apple Account. The developer cannot view your private iCloud records. You can turn off sync in Settings. Apple processes purchases, trial receipts and restore requests through the App Store.

Sharing and deletion

You choose whether to export or share. A job setup can contain a job name, wage, tip-out rules and overtime settings. It never carries shift records. Shared setup links put the code in the URL fragment, which the page reads on your device. The help site is hosted by GitHub Pages and includes no analytics script.

Deleted shifts can be restored for 30 days. Older deleted records are reduced to deletion markers for sync. Existing exported copies are controlled by whoever has the file.

Help

Log and edit a shift

Tap Log shift, enter card tips, then tap Save. Add cash or change hours as needed. Set automatic tip-out rules in Settings → Jobs. Use the calendar to find another day. Tap a saved shift to edit it. Swipe left to delete it.

Exports and backups

Settings → Your data offers CSV export and a portable .tipsheet backup. Open the backup from Files or choose Restore backup in Settings. In Insights → Tax report, pick a year to export a CSV and two PDFs for your records.

Trial and purchase

Pro is available for your first 30 days in the paid version. Logging, viewing, editing and exporting your records stay free. If you already bought Pro, use Settings → Try or buy Pro → Restore purchases.

隐私政策

小费本把你输入的班次、工作和设置保存在 iPhone。不要求注册，不接入广告、统计或追踪，也没有开发者运营的记录服务器。

开启同步且 iCloud 可用时，账本会同步到你 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库。开发者无法访问。你可在设置里关闭同步。购买、试用凭证和恢复购买由 App Store 处理。

导出或分享由你发起。工作设置口令可包含工作名称、基础时薪、分账和加班规则。班次记录不会随口令分享。口令位于链接片段，由设备读取。帮助页托管在 GitHub Pages，没有统计脚本。

删除的班次保留 30 天供恢复，之后同步仅保留删除标记。已经导出的文件由持有者管理。

使用帮助

点「记一班」，输入刷卡小费，再点「保存」。有现金小费或工时变化时可一起修改。在「设置 → 工作管理」添加自动分账规则。日历可补记；点已保存的班次可编辑，左滑可删除。

在「设置 → 你的数据」可导出 CSV 或 .tipsheet 备份。从「文件」打开备份，或在设置中选择「恢复备份」。「统计 → 报税记录」可按年导出 CSV 和两份 PDF。

收费版前 30 天开放买断版功能。记班、查看、编辑和导出记录永久免费。已买断的用户可在付费页选择「恢复购买」。
